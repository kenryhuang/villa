`resident_yun.glb` and `art/blender/characters/resident_yun.blend` are adapted from **Sintel Lite 2.57b**, by **BenDansie**.

The same attribution also applies to the rebuilt `farmer_ahe.glb`, `lao_li.glb`, `xuezhe_lin.glb`, `../farm3d/player_farmer.glb`, their corresponding editable Blender files, and the shared Sintel-derived skin, mouth, eye, glove and boot textures. Additional changes for the four village characters (2026-09-12): individual body, jaw and facial proportions; rebuilt continuous shirts and trousers; role-specific hats, kerchief, apron, waistcoat, spectacles, backpack, ledger and other accessories; reshaped hair; new shared fabric atlas; updated skin weights and four animation clips.
